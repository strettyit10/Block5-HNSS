Five rounds. 256 questions.

Every Bell Ringer plays the same way: stations alternate between players, a miss opens a steal for the other player, and each answer is followed by the explanation and a high-yield pearl. Rounds I and III carry the lab specimen photos; II, IV and V are text only.
