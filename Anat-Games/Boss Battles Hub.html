Head, Neck & Special Senses · two-player co-op · pick a deck

Five decks. Seven bosses each.

Team up against one boss per region, ending with the Cranial Nerve Hydra. Each deck is a full campaign on its own slice of the 320-question pool, sorted by yield: Deck 1 is the material most likely to be tested, Deck 5 the edge cases. Every question in a deck is asked once per campaign, so nothing is skipped.

Strike

Correct = 20 damage, +10 answered fast, +5 on a team streak of three.

Take hits

A miss lets the boss hit you. Below half health it enrages: harder hits, shorter timer.

Assist & items

Your teammate gets one shot after your miss. One hint per boss, one potion per run.

Spoils

Questions left after a boss falls still get asked — each one right heals the team 10.

How a deck playsSeven fights, roughly 6–12 questions each. Boss health scales to the number of questions it holds, so you can miss several and still win. Lose a fight and you can retry that boss with the same questions reshuffled — a loss never costs you the questions.
